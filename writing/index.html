Positions, with the counter-argument attached

Arguments about automotive retail, CRM and customer data in Saudi Arabia and the GCC. Each one states what would change my mind.

  • 2026-08-21

    The agency model did not fail on retail format. It failed on data governance

    Volkswagen paused, JLR reverted, Ford and Lotus went back. Mercedes-Benz UK grew. Same format, opposite outcomes — which means the format was never the variable.

  • 2026-08-21

    The constraint on customer-facing AI in Saudi automotive is consent lineage, not model quality

    An AI agent that writes to CRM can only act on records with provable, documented consent behind them. In most automotive databases, nobody has measured how many of those there are.

  • 2026-08-21

    In a contracting car market, share moves to whoever loses the fewest customers

    Toyota's volume in Saudi Arabia fell 17.4% in the first half of 2026 and its share rose from 26.7% to 28.8%. Losing less was the growth strategy.

  • 2026-08-21

    With most Saudi car purchases financed, the finance step owns the lead

    The digital journey is built backwards. It starts with configuring a car and ends at affordability — which is the question the customer actually had first.
