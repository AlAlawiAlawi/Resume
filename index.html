03 Selected work

Problem, Action, Result

AI · Customer opsNoor, the AI customer-engagement agent
Problem

Routine first-line enquiries arriving around the clock across web chat, WhatsApp and social — in two languages.

Action

Launched Noor on the BMW and MINI Saudi Arabia websites on 7 July 2026, serving live new-car stock with options detail, exterior and interior imagery, installment plans and current offers, test-drive requests, service-appointment booking, general requests and complaint capture — in Arabic and English. Extended to WhatsApp on 8 August 2026, then to Facebook and Instagram, with the same behaviour on every channel and images in both directions. Built an M Power mode that switches the agent’s register when the conversation turns to M.

Result

Leading importer in the GCC running agentic AI across website widget, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram as a unified omnichannel experience — round-the-clock coverage on the channels customers actually use, with routine enquiries deflected from the human team.

Digital · SprintService appointment booking
Problem

Booking a service visit meant friction and back-and-forth, with no direct self-serve path for the customer.

Action

Delivered a direct service-appointment booking solution in a 30-day sprint, live from May 2025 — booking with no friction and immediate confirmation.

Result

An immediate, effortless self-serve booking experience that removes friction from the service journey.

Analytics · Power BIVoice-of-customer analytics at scale
Problem

Unstructured NPS feedback and sales data sitting unused, with no bilingual way to read it at executive or operational level.

Action

Built executive Power BI analysis of 80k+ NPS responses across multiple branches and touchpoints with a bilingual categorization taxonomy, alongside an analytics model on 200k+ records. A daily MTD Excel dispatches the same data, giving operations and leadership one shared language and point of view.

Result

Unstructured feedback turned into a director-level decision tool.

Marketing · CDPSaudi Arabia’s first marketing automation engine on SAP
Problem

Four brands with no unified customer record, and marketing budget being spent bidding on prospects who had already converted, or on blind conquest campaigns.

Action

Launched the first marketing automation engine in Saudi Arabia built on SAP Customer Data Platform and SAP Emarsys Customer Engagement, unifying first-party data across four brands and activating it into targeting across WhatsApp, web, web push, mobile wallet and paid social on Meta, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and TikTok — up from email and SMS alone.

Result

Real-time eligibility and suppression that stops ad bidding the moment a prospect converts, granular segmentation that lifted targeting accuracy year over year, and lookalike models built on first-party data to reach high-value leads. Across the programme, SAP reports Google ad costs down around 50% on a Ramadan campaign. Campaigns now run, in his words, “at the speed of data rather than the speed of people.”

Operations · LeadershipCRM & contact-center operating model
Problem

Inbound, outbound and back-office functions running on separate reporting with no shared staffing logic.

Action

Lead a 19-person inbound / outbound / back-office organization with merged KPI reporting and Erlang-O staffing model.

Result

One operating model with consistent KPIs and demand-matched staffing.

Strategy · RoadmapMulti-brand digitalization roadmap

Architecture · DataIntegration & data infrastructure

CRM · OEM integrationJaguar Land Rover regional CRM integration
Problem

The importer’s dealer management system and the manufacturer’s regional CRM held separate customer records, with no automated path between them.

Action

Built the automated daily feed from the Autoline DMS into Jaguar Land Rover’s MENAP regional CRM — negotiating a direct SFTP connection with IP whitelisting in place of the manufacturer’s standard VPN, and backloading 15 months of aftersales and 12 months of sales history. Apr–Dec 2014.

Result

Recognized by Jaguar Land Rover MENAP as among the first importers in the region to complete the integration. Live daily from 06:30.
